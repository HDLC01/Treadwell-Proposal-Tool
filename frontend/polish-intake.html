Your browser cannot do dictation - Chrome and Edge can. Typing in the box works exactly the same.

Dictation is your browser's own, and it needs a speech service to send the audio to. Chrome and Edge have one - Chrome's is Google's, so type it instead if that is not right for this job. Brave ships without one, so the button hears you and nothing ever comes back.
